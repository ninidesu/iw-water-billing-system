Common questions
Things people usually ask
Can't find what you need? Contact the office directly.
How do I apply for a new connection?
Fill in the online application form with your property details. You'll get a reference code immediately, and our team will contact you to schedule a site inspection.
Do I need to log in to report a leak?
No. You can submit a concern or emergency report without an account. Just describe the location and issue — we'll follow up with you.
Where do I find my Contract Account Number?
Your CAN is printed on your water bill (Statement of Account) near the top. You'll need it for in-person and e-wallet payments.
How long does a new connection take?
Most new connections are completed within 5–10 business days after the site inspection and payment. You'll be notified at each step.
Can I pay using GCash or Maya?
Yes. Search for "Isabel Waters" in the bills payment section of GCash or Maya. Confirm the biller account details with our office before your first payment.
How do I dispute a bill?
Log in and use the "Report a concern" form, or visit the office. Bring a copy of your bill and any supporting information. Disputes are typically resolved within 5 business days.
